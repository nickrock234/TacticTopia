# Layer 3 — Map geometry

Generated 2026-09-30 from `engine/mapgen.ts`, 40 maps per mode and size. Rules follow `Map Generation.md`: 3×3 territories, one landmark per element per player, 1 merchant + 1 lair per player on element landmarks, 1 gold mine or mana fountain per 2 players, 3 NPC camps per player, 10–18% impassable terrain, every base within 10 walking tiles of each element, teams within ±10% on walking distances.

Walking distance counts diagonal steps, around terrain. **Turns** = tiles ÷ tiles moved per turn with both actions spent moving: knight 4, barbarian 6, thief 8 (no boots, no roads).
